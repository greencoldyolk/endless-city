## New global rules (append to the style preamble for EVERY image)

```
The city is an EAST-ASIAN contemporary city, prosperous and well-built,
slightly upscale: clean concrete, glass and metal, present-day street
furniture of good quality — strictly REAL-WORLD, nothing sci-fi or
futuristic. Everything is intact and still quietly FUNCTIONING — lamps
lit, machines humming, systems running on their own. Any damage reads as fresh
weather marks — rain-streaked, water-stained, damp-curled — never as
decades of aging: no yellowed walls, no sun-bleached surfaces. It is simply untended:
nature has moved in and taken over the gardening. Moss and vines grow
lush, but nothing is broken, rusted-through, crumbling, shabby or poor —
this is overgrowth, NOT decay. Surfaces may show gentle wear from human
use (worn-smooth wood, faded paint edges), never structural ruin.
Think globalized upscale East-Asian metropolis: spacious streets and
modern international architecture with Western-influenced contemporary
elements are welcome. Avoid the two extremes only: NO classical/historic
European scenery (Haussmann facades, Victorian lamp posts, stone
balustrades, brick brownstones, rooftop water tanks), and NO hyper-dense
cramped pigeon-cage housing blocks. Vines climb only the edges
and corners of man-made objects — machines, benches and signs stay
mostly visible, never swallowed. Window lights lean sparse: most windows
dark.
```
